# CSST Website — Staff Update Email

This is **limited on purpose**. Email only handles three things:

1. New / updated / removed **staff**
2. **Open house / info night** dates
3. Occasional **school closed** or **late start** banners

Nothing else (FAQ, photos, page copy, design) goes through this email.

---

## Status

The update system is **built into the site**. It is **not live** until the school creates the mailbox and turns it on (see `HANDOVER.md`).

**When it’s on, send updates to:** `updates@csrockets.org`  
*(or the address IT creates)*

**Who can send:** Only a short allow-list of school emails. Unknown senders are ignored.

**How fast:** About 2 minutes. You’ll get a confirmation reply.

---

## 1. School closed / late start

Shows as a banner at the top of the site. Always include an **expires** date so it disappears automatically.

```
Subject: Announcement

School is closed Monday, January 12 due to weather.
Expires January 13.
```

```
Subject: Announcement

Late start Tuesday, January 13 — first bell at 10:00 AM.
Expires January 14.
```

---

## 2. Open house / info night dates

```
Subject: New event

Add an info night on November 13 at 6:00 PM.
Location: 3650 North Nevada Ave
Note: Enter on the west side at the CSST entrance
Description: Meet staff, tour the facility, and learn about CSST.
```

```
Subject: Event update

Change the November 13 info night to November 20.
```

```
Subject: Cancel event

Remove the February 5 open house — that date is full.
```

---

## 3. Staff

```
Subject: New staff

Add to faculty:
Name: Mr. James Smith
Title: Mathematics & Data Science
Bio: Mr. Smith joined from the Air Force Academy. He holds an MS in Applied Mathematics from UCCS.
Email: james.smith@csrockets.org
```

```
Subject: Staff update

Update Coach Rivera's title to Athletic Director & STEM Coach
```

```
Subject: Staff update

Remove Sarah Johnson from faculty. She left in December.
```

If you don’t include a photo URL, a placeholder is used until a real photo is added.

---

## Tips

- Be specific: date, time, and what to do.
- One email can do more than one thing (announcement + open house).
- You’ll get a reply. If something is unclear, it will ask instead of guessing.
- Don’t email this address for Instagram, FAQ, or design changes.
